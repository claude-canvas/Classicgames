Last Light

a mouse, a lantern, and the end of the day

Every evening the lantern-caps at the feet of the old pines must be woken with fireflies, so the forest's small creatures can find their way home in the dark. Tonight the old lamplighter is ill, and the job falls to Wick, a harvest mouse no taller than a pine cone.

Gather fireflies from the long grass and carry them to the seven lantern-caps, three to each, before night falls. And keep to the grass when Old Hush the owl is hunting.

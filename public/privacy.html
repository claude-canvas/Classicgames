Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Classic Games Arcade ("we", "this site") is a collection of free browser games. This page explains what information is involved when you use the site.

No accounts

You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Game progress saved in your browser

Some games save high scores, settings and progress using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Server and hosting logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

Third-party services

  • Fonts: some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded.
  • AI companion: if you chat with the AI companion in the voxel world game, your messages are sent to an AI provider to generate replies. Don't share personal information in those messages.

Cookies and advertising

The site does not currently set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add advertising or analytics in the future, this policy will be updated before they go live to explain what is collected and how you can opt out.

Children

The games are suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.

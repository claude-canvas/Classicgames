How to fish

Cast, wait, strike

CastPress and hold on the water — the ring grows toward where you point. Let go to cast.
WaitShadows under the water are fish. Small dips are nibbles; wait for the big plunge.
StrikeWhen the "!" appears, click or press Space straight away.
Play the fishHold to reel, let go to give line. Keep the needle in the green — red snaps the line.
Where & whenEach fish has a favourite spot and hour. The journal gives hints for the ones you're missing.
LuresNew lures unlock as your journal fills. Some fish only come to the glow lure at night.

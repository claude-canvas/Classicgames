# Blackmoor: Lights Out — design & hand-off document

> The arcade's old "Raycaster" maze, rebuilt as a first-person survival-horror game.
> You wake in Room 13 of the abandoned Blackmoor Asylum during a storm. The power is out, and something that
> used to be the head nurse walks the halls. Find a light, get out of the locked ward, recover three fuses,
> restart the generator and escape through the main gate before it finds you.
> Everything lives in `public/raycaster/`. The `<!-- seo:head -->` block in index.html is generated by
> `seo/build.js`, so it is not edited by hand.

## 1. Tasks (the objective line at the top left)
1. **Find a light.** It is pitch black except for the lightning through the window. The flashlight is on the
   desk in Room 13.
2. **Get out of the ward.** The ward door at the east end of the corridor is locked (yellow).
   - The yellow key is in the last room at the dark west end.
   - Taking it triggers the first scare: the lights flicker, there are whispers, and a figure stands at the end
     of the corridor and vanishes when the flashlight touches it.
3. **Restore the power.** The note in the main hall explains that the gate is electric and the generator needs
   3 fuses. The fuses are in:
   - the **Chapel** (on the altar);
   - the **Morgue** (in the far body drawer bay);
   - the **Operating Theatre**, behind the red door. The red key is in the Records Office.
4. **The Matron wakes** when you take your first fuse: a roar, then she starts hunting. She grows faster with
   each fuse.
5. **Insert the fuses** into the generator in the Boiler Room, one press of E per fuse. Each insertion clanks
   loudly and attracts her.
6. **Escape.** With all three fuses in, the generator roars, the red alarm lights come on and the gate unlocks.
   The Matron goes straight for you. Run to the gate in the Lobby.

Checkpoints: the game saves when you pick up the flashlight, the key and each fuse. Dying restarts you at the
last checkpoint with what you carried then.

## 2. The Matron (AI, `monster.js`)
- **States:**
  - `dormant` until the first fuse;
  - `wander`: a random reachable room centre via BFS;
  - `investigate`: walks to a noise;
  - `chase`: BFS path to you, re-planned every 0.25 s;
  - `search`: looks around the last place she saw you for 6 s.
- **Sight:** grid line of sight, a ±70° cone and a range of 13 cells. The range rises to 19 if your flashlight is
  on and you face her. She always notices you within 2.5 cells.
- **Hearing:**
  - sprinting: radius 11;
  - walking: 4;
  - crouching: 0;
  - doors: 7;
  - generator: 30.
- **Speed:** wander 1.25, chase 3.0 + 0.25 per fuse (after the generator, 3.6). You walk at 2.4, sprint at
  4.0 with 4 s of stamina, and crouch at 1.2.
- **Lockers (L):** stand in front of one and press E to hide. She cannot see you inside, unless she was chasing
  and saw you climb in less than 2 s before, in which case she drags you out.
- **Catching you:** within 0.75 cells. A full-screen jump-scare (face plus scream), then the death screen.
- **Tells:** heavy dragging footsteps, panned and muffled by walls, a heartbeat that speeds up with proximity,
  the flashlight flickering when she is within 8 cells, and a "she's close" vignette.

## 3. Player and controls
- WASD to move, mouse to look (pointer lock), Shift to sprint, C to crouch, E to interact or pick up or hide,
  F for the flashlight.
- Touch: a left move stick, right-side drag to look, and buttons for E, F, sprint and crouch.
- The flashlight battery drains in about 160 s of use. Batteries (+) restore 50 %. Below 15 % it flickers and
  dims.
- HUD (minimal and diegetic):
  - the objective at top left;
  - item icons (keys, fuses) at bottom left;
  - battery and stamina bars;
  - the centre prompt `[E] …`;
  - toasts for pickups.
- Notes (n): 6 pages of story shown on a paper overlay. Reading pauses the game.

## 4. Rendering (`renderer.js`, software raycaster on a low-res buffer)
- The internal resolution is about 480 px wide. It is drawn to a canvas and scaled up with crisp pixels, which
  gives a lo-fi analogue-horror look.
- Walls use DDA. Doors are slabs set into the middle of their cell and slide sideways when they open.
  Textures are procedurally generated at 64×64: hospital plaster, tile, brick, wood, window, locker, generator,
  gate, three blood-writing walls, doors, floor linoleum and ceiling tiles.
- Floors and ceilings are cast per pixel row.
- **Lighting:**
  - **Flashlight:** a screen-space cone with distance falloff and sway.
  - **Lightmap:** each cell's RGB comes from point lights:
    - moonlight windows (with lightning flashes);
    - red emergency lamps (flickering);
    - chapel candles;
    - ceiling lamps once the power is back.
  - The lightmap is bilinear-sampled per pixel, and everything beyond the light falls to black.
- Sprites: items, decor (beds, wheelchairs, gurneys, IV stands, candles, pews) and the Matron with walk and lunge
  frames. They are depth-tested against a per-column z-buffer and lit the same way.
- **Post-processing:**
  - film grain;
  - vignette;
  - scanline flicker;
  - chromatic split and shake on scares;
  - a red pulse when she is near.

## 5. Audio (`audio.js`, all Web Audio, nothing to download)
Wind and rain drone with thunder, a sub-bass dread drone that rises with danger, and a heartbeat. Player
footsteps change with surface and speed. The Matron's footsteps and breathing are positioned with a stereo
panner and muffled by occlusion. Also: whispers (formant noise), door creaks, item pickups, scare stingers (a
dissonant string cluster), a scream, the generator spinning up and the alarm.

## 6. Files
`DESIGN.md` · `index.html` (UI and CSS; the seo:head block is generated by seo/build.js) ·
`js/main.js` (world, lightmap, player, input, interaction, scripted beats, checkpoints, UI, loop) · `js/map.js`
(map, notes, objectives, zones) · `js/textures.js` (procedural textures, sprites, the jump-scare face) ·
`js/renderer.js` (software raycaster) · `js/monster.js` (the Matron) · `js/audio.js`.
The old maze files (config.js, game.js, input.js, player.js, raycaster.js) were replaced by these.

## 7. Debug
- `?skip=N` starts at checkpoint N.
- `?god`: the Matron cannot kill you.
- `?bright`: full-bright, for checking the level.
- `window.__horror()` returns the current state.
- `?debug` exposes `window.__dbg()`: the monster, player, world, game, the interaction functions and the renderer.

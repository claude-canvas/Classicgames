How to play

Eight ball

Break, then claim solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15) with your first pot. Sink your group, then the 8 to win.

AimDrag on the table — the dotted line shows where the cue ball meets the first ball and where that ball will go.
ShootPull the cue on the right down and let go. Or hold Space and release.
SpinDrag the red dot: top for follow, bottom to draw back, sides to swerve off cushions.
FoulsScratching, hitting the wrong group first or hitting nothing gives your opponent ball in hand.

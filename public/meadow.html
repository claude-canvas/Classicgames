Meadowhop

Winter is coming. Gather carrots hidden in the tall grass and carry them home to the burrow before the sun sets.

  • Follow the butterfly. It always drifts to the nearest carrot.
  • Watch the wind. Strong gusts flatten the grass and make carrot tops glint.
  • Mind the hawk. When its shadow comes for you, hide in tall grass or run home.
  • You can carry 3 carrots at a time. Each day needs more, and the seasons turn every two days.
  • Earn clovers from stars, quests and four-leaf clovers, and spend them at Hazel's shop, the old stump in the meadow.
